The [Copernicus Land Monitoring Service data policy](https://land.copernicus.eu/en/data-policy) provides open access with source acknowledgement, identification of adaptations and no implication of EU endorsement. This general policy does not establish the exact provenance or terms of every file in this course bundle. No particular CLC year or DOI has been invented for this extract.

For a confirmed CLMS-derived output, the required acknowledgement should include: "Generated using European Union's Copernicus Land Monitoring Service information", with the relevant dataset reference once identified.

## Publication permission

On 11 November 2026, the project author reported that the course instructor confirmed that these projects and their supplied data may be published on GitHub. Publication is based on that confirmation. No blanket open licence is assigned to third-party datasets.

The complete project and data are available in the repository's Releases. The teaching handout and the lecturer's example project are not included.
